# Churvox public-copy originality audit

Date: 24 July 2026

## Decision

The current Churvox public story remains Churvox-specific:

- Churvox prepares the admin; the owner reviews and approves.
- The product is organised around an owner control room rather than a copied competitor page structure.
- Nothing important auto-sends, charges, syncs or changes records without owner approval.
- The public trial remains 14 days with no card upfront.

## Exact-phrase checks completed

The following current Churvox lines were searched as exact phrases on the public web:

- “Your business handled. Your decisions waiting.”
- “Four questions. No dashboard hunting.”
- “Churvox prepares the jobs, messages, quotes, invoices and follow-ups.”

The returned results did not identify another service-business software website using those exact lines. This is a practical originality check, not a legal trademark or copyright opinion.

## Permanent repository guard

`scripts/churvox-public-copy-originality-contract.cjs` now checks the public marketing sources for:

1. competitor names leaking into public product copy;
2. unfinished website-template residue;
3. loss of Churvox’s owner-control and no-silent-action promises;
4. loss of the 14-day, no-card trial wording;
5. duplicated H1 headlines across the main public pages; and
6. removal of the distinctive Churvox homepage lines.

The guard deliberately checks Churvox’s own product truth. It does not import, store or imitate another company’s page copy.

## Writing rule going forward

Public copy must begin with the real Churvox workflow, user problem and approval boundary. Competitor pages may be researched for broad market understanding, but their wording, section sequence, examples, screenshots, visual compositions and product claims must not be copied.
