## First seven days

### Day 1

- Publish the best launch post on the Churvox Facebook page and LinkedIn.
- Share the community version only in groups where business promotion or feedback requests are allowed.
- Send warm introductions only to people or businesses with a genuine connection.

### Day 2

- Publish one trade-specific post for lawn care, landscaping and cleaning.
- Reply to every genuine comment without pushing for a signup.

### Day 3

- Publish the founder-story version.
- Ask two trusted contacts for one referral each.

### Day 4

- Review applications in Churvox HQ.
- Send accepted, information-needed or waiting-list replies.

### Day 5

- Publish a product-explanation post: “Churvox prepares the admin. The owner checks and approves.”
- Share the short post on Instagram.

### Day 6

- Follow up once with warm contacts who expressed interest but did not apply.
- Do not follow up with people who ignored or declined the first message.

### Day 7

- Check which tracked link produced applications.
- Repeat the strongest source and stop using channels that created visits but no suitable applicants.

## Guardrails

- Do not buy email lists or send bulk unsolicited email.
- Do not post repeatedly in the same group.
- Do not claim Churvox has customers, results or savings that are not proven.
- Do not promise automatic actions; owner approval remains central.
- Do not mention other products in Churvox promotional copy.
- Do not change the locked public pricing.
