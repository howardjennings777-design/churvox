# Churvox AI Operator OS Direction Lock

This is the product direction to keep.

## Core promise

Churvox watches the business, prepares the admin, and the owner approves.

## What Churvox is

Churvox is an AI Operator OS for trade and service businesses.

It is not a generic job app. It is not a plain dashboard. It is not a busy admin system.

## Locked structure

### Smart Hub
The Smart Hub is the AI command centre.

It should show:
- AI hero message
- Owner Approval Queue
- Proof to Paid
- Today’s work
- Money watch
- Crew/dispatch support

Do not turn Smart Hub into a wall of random cards.

### AI Work Queue
This is where the owner reviews, edits, and approves prepared AI actions.

Every action should be approval-first.

### Workspaces
Jobs, Clients, Crew, Quotes, Invoices, Import, Plans, Billing, Settings.

These should be clean workspaces with lists, filters, and pop-up/detail drawers. They should not all become giant dashboards.

## Visual rules

Keep:
- Dark navy AI command-centre theme
- Cyan glow
- Floating Churvox C logo
- Rounded premium cards
- Clear owner approval language

Avoid:
- More random dashboard boxes
- Mixed old/new layouts
- Big helper widgets blocking content
- Demo-looking sections
- Tiny cramped 4-column card walls
- Full page jumps for detail views where a modal/sheet would work

## Product language

Use this line as the north star:

AI prepares the admin. You approve the work.
