# Churvox customer-ready plan

## Core promise

Job done. Admin prepared. Owner approves.

## What customers want

- Fast setup.
- Clear daily view.
- Simple scheduling.
- Mobile worker actions.
- Photos, notes, proof, and time.
- Completed work becoming invoice-ready.
- Simple follow-ups.
- Clear customer communication.
- Clear pricing.
- Human support when stuck.

## What customers hate

- Surprise pricing.
- Confusing add-ons.
- Weak mobile flow.
- Sync issues.
- Too many dashboards.
- Slow support.
- Hidden limits.
- AI that feels risky or automatic.

## Churvox strongest angle

Other job apps store the admin.
Churvox prepares the admin and waits for owner approval.

## Whole-site flow to lock

1. Smart Hub shows today, late work, approvals, and money waiting.
2. Clients are easy to add/import/edit.
3. Jobs are easy to create, assign, schedule, edit, and complete.
4. Worker view stays simple: acknowledge, start, pause, resume, note/photo proof, complete.
5. Completed paid work becomes invoice-ready.
6. Command only shows decisions: found, prepared, why, proof, approve/edit/park.
7. Money shows drafts, unpaid, overdue, and admin debt.
8. Accounting Sync stays draft-only and owner-approved.
9. Support feels human.
10. Plans are clear and honest.

## Remove before launch

- Public test pages.
- Public test CSV data.
- Public deploy-check clutter.
- Top 9 wording in customer UI.
- Launch checklist/report wording in customer UI.
- Build/test wording in customer UI.
- Old MYOB sales wording.
- Fake demo stats pretending to be live.
- Worker access to owner-only tools.
