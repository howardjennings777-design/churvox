Questions from New Zealand businesses

Is Churvox only available in New Zealand?

No. Churvox prioritises New Zealand and Australia in its public setup and examples, but businesses worldwide can use the web app.

Does Churvox send invoices automatically?

Churvox prepares admin for review. Important messages, money steps, syncs and record changes remain under owner approval.

Does Churvox replace an accountant?

No. It helps organise job and invoice information and provides draft or export handoffs. The business owner and their accounting adviser remain responsible for accounting and tax decisions.
