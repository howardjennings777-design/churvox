Questions Australian service businesses ask about Churvox

Is Churvox only for tradies?

No. It is designed for service businesses that manage clients, jobs, workers and money steps, including lawn care, landscaping, cleaning, property maintenance, handyman work, painting, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, pest control and other mobile service businesses.

Can Churvox manage recurring jobs?

Yes. Recurring work is part of the job workflow so regular visits can stay connected to the client, worker, notes, proof and billing history.

Does Churvox support GST?

Churvox supports configurable GST in the quote and invoice workflow. Each business remains responsible for checking its own tax and accounting treatment.

Can Churvox connect accounting work?

Churvox supports owner-controlled Xero draft invoice handoff where connected and practical export options for an accounting workflow or bookkeeper.

Does Churvox automatically send invoices or messages?

The core approach is owner control. Churvox prepares admin and surfaces decisions; important sends, money steps and record changes remain available for owner review and approval.
