For service businesses where the work happens away from the desk.

Running a mobile service team usually means the same information moves through several stages: a client asks for work, someone schedules it, a worker attends, proof comes back, extras need checking and the job eventually becomes an invoice. Churvox keeps those stages tied to the same client and job so the owner is not rebuilding the story from separate messages, notes and spreadsheets.

Capture the customer and the job once

Keep the address, contact details, scope, access notes, timing and price with the client and job record instead of retyping them through the workflow.

Schedule recurring and one-off work

Get field updates back into the record

Keep approval with the owner

Hand cleaner records to accounting

Questions about service business software in Australia

What sort of business is Churvox designed for?

Churvox is aimed at service businesses that manage clients, scheduled work, field workers and billing. That includes recurring service businesses, quoted project work and mobile technical trades.

Can I use Churvox for recurring jobs?

Yes. One-off and recurring work can stay connected to the client, worker, notes, proof and billing history so repeat work does not start from zero each visit.

Does Churvox support GST?

Churvox supports configurable GST in quote and invoice workflows. Businesses remain responsible for checking their own tax and accounting treatment.

Does Churvox automatically send invoices or customer messages?

The core approach is owner control. Churvox prepares routine admin and surfaces decisions; important sends, money steps and record changes remain available for owner review and approval.

Is Churvox only available in Australia?

No. Churvox focuses its public experience on New Zealand and Australia but is available to service businesses worldwide.
