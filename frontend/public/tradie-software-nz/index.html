NZ tradie software questions

What does tradie software actually replace?

It can reduce the need to rebuild job information across calendars, messages, notes, photos, spreadsheets and invoice drafts by keeping those records connected around the client and job.

Can Churvox handle recurring jobs?

Yes. Recurring jobs are part of the workflow, making it suitable for regular rounds and repeat service visits as well as one-off work.

Does Churvox support NZ GST?

Churvox supports configurable GST and uses NZD public pricing. The business remains responsible for checking its own tax and accounting treatment.

Is there a free version?

The public offer is a 14-day free trial with no card required upfront. Current paid plans start at NZ$39 per month + GST.

Will Churvox send things without the owner checking?

The product is built around prepared admin and owner approval. Important sends, money steps and record changes remain visible for owner review.
