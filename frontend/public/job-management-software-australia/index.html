Australian job management software questions

What problems does job management software solve?

It reduces double handling between client details, calendars, worker communication, notes, proof, quotes and invoices by keeping those records attached to the work they belong to.

Can Churvox manage recurring work?

Yes. Churvox supports recurring jobs so repeat service visits can retain the relevant client, worker, notes, proof and billing context.

Does Churvox support GST?

Churvox supports configurable GST in the quote and invoice workflow. Businesses remain responsible for checking their own tax and accounting treatment.

Is Churvox available outside Australia?

Yes. Churvox prioritises New Zealand and Australia in its public setup and business language but the web app is available worldwide.
