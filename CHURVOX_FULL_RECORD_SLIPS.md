# Churvox Full Record Slips

The slip is now the owner-facing record.

## What changed
- Slip popup is full screen.
- Slip includes client, job, invoice, quote, payment, worker, message, line items, checks, missing fields and raw details.
- Owner edits details inside the slip.
- Approve button runs from the slip.
- Old record buttons are hidden/renamed from normal screens.
